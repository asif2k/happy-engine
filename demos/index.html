happy_system demos

One page for each feature of the system. Each is a real page of the engine: it draws in WebGL2 and its numbers are in window.happy_demo. The pictures below were taken from the pages themselves in headless Chrome (software rendering, so they run at about 8 fps there; a real GPU is much faster).

The pages are bundled and served by the engine's own dev server. From the repo root:

node packing.js -serve default
# then open http://localhost:8099/demos/index.html

Creatures

World: terrain, city, vegetation

Engine: rendering, physics, particles

AI

Every page hides the engine's own numbers panel; add ?stats=1 to show it. Left drag turns the camera, right drag pans, the wheel zooms; the terrain and city pages also fly with W A S D (Shift: fast), Q / E down / up. People are drawn from the palette (no painted skin). emu.js and ostrich.js in system/creatures/ do not mesh (an sdf error in the files) and are left out.
